WHO WE ARE

A family of believers in the heart of Luzira

St. Stephen's Luzira C.O.U is a parish of the Church of Uganda: families, workers, youth and children who love the Lord, love his Word and love our neighbours.

Our worship is rooted in Scripture and the Anglican tradition, and lived out with prayer, warmth and a welcome for everyone.

What to expect

Reverent, joyful worship, the Word of God faithfully preached, and a community ready to welcome you. That is a typical Sunday at St. Stephen's Luzira.

Our singing, prayers and sacraments draw the whole congregation in. While you worship, children and teens are taught in their own Sunday school and youth services, with care suited to their age.

From the moment you arrive, you will find an atmosphere of love and the presence of God. We cannot wait to worship with you.
