LATEST SERMON

Workers’ Evening

Counsel. Byron Mwanje · Genesis 39

Theme: Faithful Stewardship at Work. Drawing from the life of Joseph in Genesis 39, this message looks at what it means to serve God faithfully in the workplace: staying honest and diligent, even when no one is watching or when circumstances are unfair.

  • God’s presence goes with us into every workplace
  • Integrity matters most when it is tested
  • Faithful work honours God and blesses others
